= Scan a light receipt (ALDI / thermal)

== Goal

Read faint black text on cheap thermal paper without the driver deciding a global cutoff on a postage-stamp preview.

== Steps

. Put the receipt on the **platen glass**, not the ADF if the paper curls. Close the lid or cover with a white sheet.
. In PolyglotScan, choose the scanner (WIA or TWAIN — TWAIN is usually the Epson Scan 2 source).
. Set DPI to 300 (600 if the type is tiny). Image type stays **grayscale**.
. Click *Preview*. Wait for the full-window image.
. Wheel-zoom into the feeblest line of type. Right-drag for a loupe.
. Drag **Threshold**. If the background speckles before the text fills, enable **Sauvola** (local contrast) instead of a single cutoff.
. *Scan* (or skip if preview resolution is enough) and *Save* as PNG or PDF. JPEG-XL if you want a small lossless/near-lossless archive.

== If the scanner is busy / WIA hangs

WIA device listing can block on a sleeping MFP. Use TWAIN, unplug extra cameras, or *File → Open image* on a scan you already made and re-binarize it here.
